# The Visiteur owns a cookie Panier; the Compte client is demanded at Checkout

Adding to the Panier required an active session: an anonymous shopper clicking "Ajouter au panier" was bounced off the product page into the Connexion modal and the article was never added — a conversion leak at the funnel's widest point, and a contradiction of ADR-0001's own "never leave the page". We decided a Visiteur may assemble a Panier held in an unsigned httpOnly cookie (`[{variantId, quantity}]`, ≤ 20 distinct articles, every variant revalidated against the database), rendered by `/panier` exactly like a Client's persisted Panier; the Compte client is demanded only at Checkout. At authentication the cookie replays through the existing `addToCart` rules — quantities summed per variant, stale articles skipped best-effort — then the cookie is deleted, keeping `getCart` single-source.

Why: this shop's traffic arrives cold from shared links (share previews), and cash-on-delivery buyers are often one-time purchasers; demanding account creation before an add-to-cart loses the sales the persisted cart was built to capture. A cookie cart achieves guest accumulation without touching the Prisma schema. Signed cookies were considered and rejected — the server revalidates every variant, so a forged cookie harms only its carrier.
